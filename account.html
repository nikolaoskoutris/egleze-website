Your individual reactions stay private — never shown to other readers, never linked to your name. You'll see how the whole community reacted to each story, but no one sees how you did.

We hold this as a principle, not just a feature. When every reaction is public, people learn to perform for their audience instead of being honest with themselves — and self-censorship sets in. We refuse to build that pattern into Egleze. Your reactions are private because honest engagement requires the freedom to react without an audience.
