Add Egleze to your home screen

A fast, full-screen way to read the day's most important moments — no app-store download, no clutter. Takes about ten seconds.

On your iPhone or iPad

Safari
  1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Tap Add in the top corner. Egleze now lives on your home screen.

On your Android phone

Chrome
  1. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm with Install. Egleze now lives on your home screen.
