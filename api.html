Podcast intelligence, structured and attributed.

Egleze reads thousands of hours of independent podcasts and surfaces the moments that matter. The Egleze API will open that layer to developers, researchers, and platforms — every moment tagged by topic, dated, and linked back to its source.

A glimpse of what we're building (illustrative):
