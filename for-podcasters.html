What being on Egleze means

When a moment from your show earns its place, we report who said it, cite your podcast by name, and point listeners to the full conversation on your channel. We're a discovery layer, not a replacement — every clip is a doorway back to you.

  • Attribution, always. Your show name and a link to the full episode travel with every moment.
  • New audiences. People who'd never have found a three-hour episode discover it through a single moment that mattered.
  • Editorial, not algorithmic. Moments are chosen by editors for substance, not engagement bait.
  • You stay in control. Questions, corrections, or opt-out — we're a message away.
